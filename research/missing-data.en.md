# Technical data to request

[🇫🇷 Version française](missing-data.md) | [🇬🇧 English](missing-data.en.md)

The following information is needed to move from a preliminary estimate to a meaningful performance assessment.

| Required data | Why it matters | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Exact site location | Identify site conditions and solar resource | To confirm |
| Borehole depth | Understand the installation | Unknown |
| Static water level | Establish the water level before pumping | Unknown |
| Dynamic water level | Establish the water level while pumping | Unknown |
| Total dynamic head (TDH) | Determine the actual pumping requirement | Unknown |
| Pipe diameter, length and fittings | Estimate hydraulic losses | Unknown |
| Pump make and model | Identify equipment characteristics | Unknown |
| Pump curve and rated power | Check the flow/head operating point | Unknown |
| Controller / variable-speed drive | Understand electrical control and limits | Unknown |
| Actual flow rate | Compare delivered flow with the target | Unknown |
| Daily operating hours | Calculate actual daily output and energy | Unknown |
| Tank capacity | Understand storage and operating cycle | Unknown |
| Measured daily water volume | Verify the reported 50 m³/day | Unknown |
| PV module/controller configuration | Confirm the reported 15 kWp and system architecture | Unknown |
| PV production records | Compare available solar energy with demand | Unknown |
| Seasonal operating records | Assess variation throughout the year | Unknown |

## Suggested questions for the operator
1. Can you share the pump make/model, rated power and pump curve?
2. What are the static and dynamic water levels, and what total dynamic head was used for sizing?
3. Is the 50 m³/day figure a measured average, a design target, or a maximum?
4. How many hours per day does the pump operate, and what flow rate is measured?
5. What controller is installed, and is there any backup supply or energy storage?
6. Are daily water-volume and PV-production records available?

Record the source and date for each answer. Do not replace unknown values with assumptions without clearly labelling them.
